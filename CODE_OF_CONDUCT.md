# Code of Conduct

Contributions, bug reports, and reviews are welcome. Keep the space friendly:

- Be patient and kind. This is a small hobby project maintained in spare time.
- Assume good intent, and critique code rather than people.
- No harassment, spam, or personal attacks. People who behave this way may have
  their contributions moderated or removed.

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
that most open-source communities adopt.
